# Fight rate: where tank items' extra health goes (Phantom+)

74,338 Phantom+ matches (patch 09-29-2026, 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-24), each player's stat snapshots every ~3-5 min (sim/scripts/fight_rate/fetch_rates.py, fit_fight_rate.py). Per interval: damage dealt to heroes, damage taken, deaths and kills + assists per minute.

## Max health elasticity

Within (hero, net worth // 2500, game time // 300 s): % change of each rate per % more max health (SE: jackknife over 10 match groups).

| lane | 3,229,919 | +0.52 ± 0.00 | +0.37 ± 0.00 | -0.70 ± 0.01 | +0.68 ± 0.00 |
| mid | 2,618,032 | +0.56 ± 0.00 | +0.17 ± 0.00 | -0.47 ± 0.01 | +0.40 ± 0.01 |
| late | 1,032,436 | +0.71 ± 0.01 | -0.10 ± 0.01 | -0.46 ± 0.01 | +0.22 ± 0.02 |

More max health is spent, not banked: players with more health take more damage per minute (more or longer fights) and join more kills, while dealing about the same damage per minute at the same net worth (the souls went into health instead of damage).
