# Item win edge (all tier 1-4 items)

227,561 ranked matches (2,730,732 players), current patch builds. Edge = win-rate points vs buyers of other same-slot same-tier items, matched on hero, 5-min purchase bin, team item-souls lead and own souls vs team (Mantel-Haenszel-style pooled difference, weights = buyers). CI = 95% normal approx. Bracket edges: low = Initiate-Arcanist, mid = Ritualist-Phantom, high = Ascendant-Eternus. `lead` = mean team item-souls lead bucket at purchase (1 = +2,500 souls); `min` = mean purchase minute.

Script: `analysis/scripts/item_win_edge.py`. Data: `analysis/out/item_win_edge.json`.
