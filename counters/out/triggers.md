# What triggers counter-item buys

Rate ratio = how much more likely a player is to buy the counter in the next 5 minutes when an enemy holds that item (or that hero is on the enemy team), versus when not, at the same moment. Pooled over the buyer's own hero and the checkpoint (Mantel-Haenszel). Checkpoints every 5 min from 5 to 40 min; exposures with 3,000+ player-checkpoints.
